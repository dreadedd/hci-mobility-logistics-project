No notes were taken during the field interviews conducted on 19 August 2026. This worksheet lets each interviewer reconstruct what they remember **from their own recollection only**.

## Interview 1

**Interviewer(s):** Obed Boateng Ayim, Emmanuella Ewurama Prah, Annabelle Naa Dedei Armah
**Approx. date/time:** ~3–5 pm, 19 August 2026
**Location on campus:** NNB car park
**Interviewee (anonymous description):** Level 300 female student

### What did you ask them about?
Whether the timing of shuttle arrivals is accurate (vs. waiting a long time not knowing when it will come), whether campus directions/navigation are clear, and whether an app showing live shuttle ETAs and accurate campus directions would be useful.

### What do you remember them saying?
- Problem(s) they mentioned: Estimated time of arrival for buses — flagged as a core feature we should build.
- Shuttles/taxis/ride-hailing apps: Arrival times are consistently inaccurate; no way to tell if a shuttle has broken down or is delayed en route.
- Finding buildings/lecture halls: Campus direction data and building info on other map apps is often inaccurate/out of date.
- Cost/fares: Fares are fairly constant — an app couldn't do much beyond displaying current prices.
- Quote/phrase that stuck: wants live tracking of shuttle locations on campus rather than a static inaccurate ETA.

### Anything else you noticed?
Not recorded.
