# Project Definition

## Project Title
Improving the User Experience of Mobility and Logistics Services in Ghana

## Domain
Mobility and Logistics

## Background
Mobility and logistics applications are widely used for ride-hailing, deliveries, navigation, and transportation services. However, users may experience difficulties such as unclear navigation, inaccurate location information, poor communication, confusing interfaces, and limited feedback during important tasks.

This project applies Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and user-centred design principles to investigate these challenges and develop an improved digital solution based on evidence gathered from real users.

## Problem Statement
Users of mobility and logistics services in Ghana may experience usability challenges when booking rides, tracking drivers or deliveries, entering locations, making payments, and receiving updates.

The project will investigate these challenges through user research and use the findings to guide the design of an improved interface.

## Target Users

The primary users may include:

- University students
- Young adults
- Workers and commuters
- Ride-hailing users
- Delivery-service users
- Drivers and riders

## Project Goal
To identify usability problems experienced by users of mobility and logistics services in Ghana and design a user-centred interface that improves clarity, efficiency, accessibility, and overall user experience.

## Research Objectives

1. Understand how people in Ghana currently use mobility and logistics applications.
2. Identify common difficulties and frustrations experienced by users.
3. Determine the features and information users consider most important.
4. Analyse existing mobility and logistics interfaces.
5. Translate research findings into user and design requirements.
6. Develop and test an improved interface based on the findings.

## Research Questions

1. Which mobility and logistics applications do users commonly use?
2. What tasks do users perform most frequently?
3. What usability problems do users encounter?
4. Which parts of the user experience cause the most frustration?
5. What improvements do users want?
6. What information should be easier to find?
7. How can the interface make important tasks faster and clearer?

## Scope

The project will focus on the digital user experience of mobility and logistics services in Ghana.

The research may examine areas such as:

- Ride booking
- Location selection
- Driver or rider communication
- Live tracking
- Delivery tracking
- Payments
- Notifications
- Navigation
- Accessibility
- Customer support

The exact problem to be solved will be refined after analysing the user research findings.

## Design Process

The project will follow this process:

1. Define the problem
2. Conduct user research
3. Analyse research findings
4. Identify user needs and pain points
5. Define design requirements
6. Create low-fidelity wireframes
7. Develop a high-fidelity prototype
8. Conduct usability testing
9. Improve the prototype
10. Present the final solution
