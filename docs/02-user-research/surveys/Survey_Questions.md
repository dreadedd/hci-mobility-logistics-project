# University of Ghana Campus Mobility Survey

## Survey Description

We are conducting this survey as part of a Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) group project to understand how students move around the University of Ghana campus and the challenges they experience with transportation and navigation.

Your responses will help us identify real user needs and guide the design of an improved campus mobility solution.

The survey is for academic purposes and should take approximately 3–5 minutes to complete.
